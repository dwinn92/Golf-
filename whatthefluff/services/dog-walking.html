A typical day

What a Tuesday looks like for your dog

Give or take a heroic puddle.

  • 10:45 — Pick-up

    I arrive, say a very embarrassing hello, harness on, quick toilet break in the garden, then into their own crate in the van.

  • 11:00 — Boots on the ground

    Out to the heath. Long-line for the recall learners, off-lead for the reliable ones, sniffing encouraged relentlessly — a good sniff tires a dog out more than a fast mile.

  • 11:40 — Water, games, more sniffing

    Recall practice with actual cheese, a paddle in the stream if it's warm, then a calm walk back to settle everyone down.

  • 12:05 — Home

    Towel-off, water bowl topped up, a tidy-up of any mud on the floor, and a message with photos before I've pulled off your drive.

  • 12:10 — The best nap of their week

    You get the photo. They get the sofa.

Dog walking questions

The things people ask before booking

How much is dog walking in Greenhithe?

£16 for a one-hour group walk, £24 for two hours, £22 for a one-hour solo walk or £16 for a 30-minute solo. Private secure field hire is £25, and a block of five group walks is £75. Collection, drop-off, towels, treats and the GPS tracking are all included in the price.

How long is a walk, really?

A full hour or a full two hours of actual walking — the clock starts when we reach the field, not when I leave my drive. Travel time is mine to absorb, not yours to pay for. The two-hour walk is the one most working owners end up on, because it genuinely empties the tank.

Where do the walks happen?

Dartford Heath, Swanscombe Heritage Park, the Thames Path at Ingress, Stone Lodge and the woods out towards Bean, plus private secure fields when a dog needs space to itself. I never use the same place more than twice in a week — it keeps walks interesting for the dogs and means nobody can predict where we'll be.

What happens in bad weather?

We still go. Dogs need the day out and I own extremely unflattering waterproofs. In genuine danger — ice, thunder, heat above 24°C — walks switch to a shorter lead walk or an in-home enrichment visit at the same price, and I'll message you first.

Is my dog insured while they're with you?

Yes. I carry full public liability plus care, custody and control cover, which is the part that actually matters when a dog is in someone else's hands. Certificates are shown at the meet & greet.

Do you walk dogs who pull, bark or aren't great with other dogs?

Constantly. Pullers get harness and loose-lead work built into the walk. Dogs who find other dogs difficult go solo on quiet routes at quiet times — and if you'd like to change that, the training sessions pick up where the walk leaves off.

Will my dog be in a van with other dogs?

Yes — in a purpose-fitted, ventilated van with individual crates, harnesses and a temperature alarm. Never loose, never crated together, never left unattended.

Can I have the same day and time every week?

That's how most families book, and regular slots get priority when the diary fills up. One-off and ad-hoc walks are welcome whenever there's space.
