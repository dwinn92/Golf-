Good questions

Everything you're about to ask

And a few things you might not think of until the first day.

How much does a dog walker cost in Greenhithe?

A one-hour group adventure walk is £16 and a two-hour walk is £24. A solo one-to-one walk is £22, and in-home cat visits and pet check-ins are £13 per 30-minute visit. A block of five group walks is £75. Everything is listed openly on the prices page; there are no booking fees, no peak-time surcharges and no lock-in contracts.

Are you insured, DBS checked and first aid trained?

All three. I hold full public liability and care-custody-control insurance, an enhanced DBS certificate, and canine first aid and CPR training that I keep current. I bring the paperwork to the meet & greet so you can see it for yourself — you shouldn't have to ask a stranger for proof before handing over a key.

How big are the group walks, and where do they go?

Small and carefully matched — by size, pace and play style, so the bouncy teenagers go out together and the gentle seniors get the calmer route. Your dog is never thrown into a group they haven't been introduced to.

Walks are one or two hours, and I never use the same place more than twice in a week. Dartford Heath, Swanscombe Heritage Park, the Thames Path at Ingress, Stone Lodge and the woods out towards Bean all get a turn — it keeps things interesting for the dogs, and it means nobody can predict where we'll be.

What happens at the free meet & greet?

I come to your home for about 30 minutes. We do the boring-but-important bits — vet details, feeding, medication, quirks, recall, the neighbour's cat they hate — and your dog gets to decide whether they like me (they usually do; I come with cheese). You'll get keys sorted, paperwork signed and a schedule agreed. It costs nothing and there's no obligation to book.

Do you look after cats and other pets too?

Yes — cat visits and small animal care have been part of this since 2020, and they're one of my favourite parts of the week. A 30-minute check-in covers feeding, fresh water, litter trays, medication, play or a lap-sit, plus post, plants, curtains and lights so the house looks lived in. Rabbits, guinea pigs, small furries and fish too. See what's included.

How will I know my pet is okay?

Every walk and visit ends with a report card in your phone: photos, where you went, how far, toilet notes, mood, food eaten and anything I noticed. Group walks are tracked live on a Tractive GPS so you can see the route, not just take my word for it.

My dog is reactive or nervous — can you still help?

Very often, yes. Reactive and anxious dogs go out solo on quiet field and canal routes at off-peak times, at their pace, with a longer settle-in period of two or three visits before anything changes. Tell me everything at the meet & greet — including the embarrassing bits. I have never once judged a dog for being a dog.

How do you get into my home?

Whatever suits you: a coded key safe (my preference, and I'll help you fit one), a key held securely, or you letting me in. Keys are tagged with a code — never your name, address or your pet's name — stored in a locked box, and handed straight back if you stop booking.

Can I book a one-off walk, or does it have to be regular?

Both work. Regular weekly slots get priority and the block-booking price; one-offs and holiday cover are welcome whenever I have space. Change or cancel with 24 hours' notice and there's no charge.

Which areas do you cover?

Greenhithe, Swanscombe, Stone, Knockhall, Ingress Park, Ebbsfleet, Northfleet, Dartford, Bean and Betsham — roughly a 15-minute drive around Greenhithe. Just outside? Check the areas page or ask; if I'm already passing your road, I can usually make it work.
