Course & Playing Handicap

Course Handicap = Index × (Slope ÷ 113) + (Course Rating − Par), rounded. Playing Handicap = Course Handicap × allowance, rounded.

Calculations follow the World Handicap System as operated by England Golf (2024 revision): best 8 of the last 20 differentials, net double bogey, PCC, exceptional score reductions and the soft/hard caps. Rounds, members and courses are shared with everyone who can open this page. This is a friends tracker — your official Handicap Index lives with your club and the England Golf / WHS platform.
