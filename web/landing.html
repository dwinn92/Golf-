What it does

📊

A real Handicap Index

Every round gets a Score Differential from the Course Rating, Slope Rating and the day's PCC. Your index is the average of the best 8 of your last 20, with the soft and hard caps and exceptional score reductions applied — and the selection table while your record is still short.

⛳

Score how you like

A total, Stableford points, or hole by hole with the net double bogey maximum enforced as you tap. Full rounds and 9-hole rounds, general play or competition. Leave mid-round and the card is still there when you come back.

🏌️

Keep the whole group's card

One person keys the fourball. Each player's strokes are capped against their own Course Handicap, not the scorer's, and each is handed their card afterwards. Accepting is what posts it — nobody can move anyone else's handicap.

✓

Cards confirmed by a marker

A playing partner named on the card can confirm they saw the score, the way a marker signs for you. You can never confirm your own.

🔒

Private clubhouses

You start in a clubhouse of your own. Share its code to bring people in, or join theirs. You see the rounds of the people you share a clubhouse with, and nobody else — enforced by the database, not just the screen.

📍

Every UK course

2,891 courses across England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland, with par and yardage where they are known. Tap once and it finds the one you are standing on.

📈

See what moved it

Which rounds are counting, what your index did after each one, how you compare with everyone in the clubhouse, and head-to-head against anyone you have played with.

📷

The detail, if you want it

Fairways, greens in regulation, putts and penalties, hole by hole — all optional. Attach a photo of the paper card. Playing handicap allowances for fourball, foursomes, greensomes and scramble.

Getting started

  1. Create an accountYou land in a private clubhouse of your own.
  2. Add your teeCourse Rating and Slope are printed on the scorecard. Enter them once and the whole clubhouse has them.
  3. Post three roundsThree scores is the minimum for an index. Twenty gives you the full picture.
  4. Bring your friends inSend them your clubhouse code.

What it is not

Fairway is a tracker for you and your friends. It is not an official record: your official Handicap Index lives with your club and on the England Golf / WHS platform, and only scores submitted there count towards it. Course and Slope Ratings are not published in any open dataset, so they come off your scorecard — Fairway will never invent them.
