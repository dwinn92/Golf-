Course & Playing Handicap calculator

Course Handicap = Index × (Slope ÷ 113) + (Course Rating − Par), rounded. Playing Handicap = Course Handicap × allowance, rounded.

Calculations follow the World Handicap System as operated by England Golf (2024 revision): best 8 of last 20 differentials, net double bogey, PCC, exceptional score reductions and the soft/hard caps. Data is stored locally in your browser. This is a personal tracker — your official Handicap Index lives with your club and England Golf / WHS platform.
